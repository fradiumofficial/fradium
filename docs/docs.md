Product Requirements Document - Fradium

1. Background
   The rapid development of the digital economy is marked by the adoption of blockchain technology and digital assets such as cryptocurrency, NFTs, and smart contracts in many countries, including Indonesia. This transformation opens up many opportunities for innovation and efficiency in digital financial transactions, but also brings new risks that cannot be ignored, ranging from fraud, asset theft, data breaches, to the rise of illegal activities such as online gambling.
   According to the Chainalysis Crypto Crime Report 2024, global losses due to crimes in the digital asset sector reached more than $24.2 billion throughout 2023. The most common types of crimes include investment fraud (crypto scams), rug pulls, phishing, and fund theft through smart contract exploitation. These threats occur not only globally but also impact the digital ecosystem in Southeast Asia and Indonesia, which is recorded as one of the fastest-growing regions for digital asset users.
   On the other hand, blockchain security literacy among the public and developers is still relatively low. Many users do not understand how to identify malicious smart contracts, verify the reputation of destination wallets, or distinguish between legitimate and illegal transactions. This condition provides a wide opportunity for cybercriminals to carry out new fraud schemes, including the misuse of blockchain for online gambling and money laundering activities.
   In this context, Risk Management & Consumer Protection becomes a crucial aspect to support the growth of a safe, trustworthy, and sustainable digital economic ecosystem. Fradium is present as an innovative solution to provide real protection for consumers, promote transparency, and enhance security in every digital asset transaction.

2. Problem Statement

   High Rate of Fraud & Blockchain Transaction Risks:
   Reports from Chainalysis and reporting platforms such as Chainabuse reveal that globally, cases of crypto fraud such as rug pulls, phishing, smart contract scams, and digital asset theft continue to increase. Losses suffered by victims reach billions of US dollars annually, often because users are unaware they have interacted with illegal wallets or smart contracts.

   Lack of Early Detection Systems and Consumer Protection:
   Most platforms only rely on passive education, without real-time detection tools that are easily accessible to the general public. As a result, victims find it difficult to identify dangerous addresses or smart contracts with vulnerabilities/backdoors.

   Lack of Community Involvement in Supervision:
   There is no centralized community reporting and validation system to update blacklists of addresses, smart contract scams, or wallets used in illegal activities (including online gambling).

   These problems result in a high rate of fraud and weak early detection systems, ultimately leading to material losses for society, decreased public trust in the digital economic ecosystem, and increased challenges for regulators and industry players in maintaining the integrity of digital asset transactions.

3. Main Features of Fradium

   A. Fraud Detection by Address
   Description:
   Fraud Detection by Address is a system that allows users to check the reputation of blockchain wallet addresses (such as Ethereum, BNB Chain, and others) before making transactions. This feature is designed to detect potential risks and prevent users from interacting with wallet addresses that have a history of suspicious or fraudulent activity.
   How It Works: - Users enter the destination wallet address they want to check. - The system automatically compares the address with an integrated blacklist database, such as Chainabuse. - The system then performs behavior analysis using machine learning to detect abnormal transaction patterns, such as transfers to many new wallets or fund flows from scam addresses. - The analysis results are presented as a risk score (Green/Yellow/Red) along with notification and recommended actions for the user.
   Benefits: - Prevents users from transferring funds to scam wallet addresses. - Significantly reduces the number of digital asset fraud victims.

   B. Smart Contract Audit with Mythril
   Description:
   This feature provides automatic audit services for Solidity-based smart contracts, which are commonly used on various public blockchains.
   How It Works: - Users can upload or paste the smart contract code, or enter the contract address to be audited. - The system will run a static analysis process using Mythril, an open-source tool to detect security vulnerabilities such as reentrancy, integer overflow/underflow, backdoors, and other logic bugs. - Audit reports are presented in two formats: - A complete technical report for developers - An easy-to-understand explanation for general users - The system also provides recommendations for improvements and security education related to the findings.
   Benefits: - Helps developers and users identify and avoid dangerous smart contracts before interacting or investing. - Improves security standards in the development and use of smart contracts in the blockchain ecosystem.

   C. Fradium Wallet
   Description:
   Fradium Wallet is a digital asset wallet (multi-chain) equipped with an automatic fraud protection system.
   How It Works: - Every time a user initiates an outgoing transaction, the system automatically checks the destination address using the Fraud Detection by Address module. - If the destination address is indicated as a scam or high risk, the user will receive an instant warning notification. - There is a layered protection mechanism, where users cannot proceed with transactions to very high-risk addresses unless done with explicit consent.
   Benefits: - Reduces the risk of human error and fraud in digital asset transactions. - Provides a sense of security and comfort for users before making transactions.

   D. Community Voting & Reporting
   Description:
   Community Voting & Reporting is a crowdsourcing feature that enables reporting, voting, and joint validation of wallet addresses or smart contracts suspected of fraud.
   How It Works: - Users can submit reports on addresses or contracts suspected of being problematic, accompanied by explanations and supporting evidence if available. - Incoming reports can be validated and voted on by other community users, so the system accumulates the reporter's reputation and the validity level of the report. - Addresses or contracts that have been validated as fraud are automatically added to the platform's global blacklist and used for subsequent detection.
   Benefits: - Accelerates the process of updating and distributing the blacklist database collectively. - Increases public participation in supervision, and builds a collaborative and transparent blockchain security ecosystem.

4. Target Users

   Fradium is designed to be used by various layers of users in the digital asset and blockchain ecosystem, with the main coverage as follows:

   - Individual/general users:
     The general public, retail investors, and digital asset users (crypto, NFT, etc.) who need protection when transacting and want to ensure security before interacting with wallets or smart contracts.
   - Developers and project owners:
     Smart contract developers, DApp creators, or blockchain project owners who need automatic audit tools and blacklist databases to ensure their ecosystem is safe from security vulnerabilities and fraud risks.
   - Blockchain community:
     Community members, educators, and digital literacy activists who are active in reporting, voting, and validating fraud data through crowdsourcing mechanisms.
   - Regulators and financial industry:
     Supervisory bodies, regulators (BI, OJK), and financial institutions that want to monitor, analyze fraud trends, and improve the integrity of digital asset transactions in Indonesia.

5. Product Objectives

   Fradium is developed to achieve the following main objectives:

   - Increase the security of digital asset transactions by providing fraud detection systems, smart contract audits, and wallet protection that are easily accessible to all user segments.
   - Provide real protection for consumers against the risks of fraud, asset theft, and illegal activities in the blockchain ecosystem through early detection and instant warnings.
   - Encourage community collaboration and participation in supervision and fraud reporting, so that the blacklist database can be updated quickly and responsively.
   - Build trust and transparency in the digital economic ecosystem, so that the public is increasingly confident to transact and innovate in the field of digital assets.
   - Support regulators and industry players in monitoring, analyzing, and taking preventive actions on emerging fraud trends and patterns, in order to maintain the integrity of the national digital financial system.
